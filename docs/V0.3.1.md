## v0.3.0 content audit

The [card-by-card audit](content-audit-0.3.0.csv) covers the 1,000 cards from release commit `914d872`. A keep has a clear joke or setup that holds up aloud. A rewrite has a usable premise buried under weak wording or an extra clause. A remove retires a repetitive, narrow or low-payoff idea.

| Card type | Keep | Rewrite | Remove |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Scenario | 45 | 155 | 50 |
| Response | 197 | 330 | 223 |

- **Nearly every setup sounds the same.** All 250 scenarios are questions and 248 start with “What”. Introduce overheard lines, fill-ins, messages and confessions. Players should hear the tension before they start looking for an answer.
- **Specific details can make a prompt a guessing game.** `scenario-211` needs a thing that lives under a sink and could plausibly be a tenant. A landlord, a sink and a tenant joke are already three decisions made for the table. The category needs broader excuses for chaos.
- **Responses often explain their own punchline.** 107 exceed 12 words, including 38 in social and 29 in expectations. `response-121` explains a medical referral where an embarrassing sound or physical reaction would do. `response-230` gives an exact appliance count without a meaningful turn. Shortening it would preserve the same weak idea, so both cards are marked remove.
- **Verbal and subject repetition hide behind unique text.** 477 responses begin with “A”, “An”, “The”, or “My”. That opening can suit a flexible noun phrase, but entire runs of overqualified props sound alike. Corporate phrasing, paperwork, funerals with extra charges, dentists and parking form repeated joke families. Limit each device and vary who is speaking, what they did, and what they were trying to conceal.
- **Keep sharp, legible lines.** A safe word neither of us could spell (`response-001`) and “I've been banned from stronger establishments” (`response-631`) create an image without explaining it. The obituary prompt (`scenario-127`) has recognizable stakes and accepts objects, admissions and dialogue.

## Verification record

- **1. Audit** reviewed all 250 scenarios and 750 responses by category, scanned length, openings and recurring subjects, and checked that the audit contains exactly one decision for each v0.3.0 card.

- **2. Humor identity** wrote and applied the six-category style guide to a calibration set of actual v0.3.0 combinations. The examples show how the review distinguishes a playable surprise from a narrow or flat pairing.
